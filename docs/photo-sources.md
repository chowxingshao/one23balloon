# Photo sources

All photos are the client's own work, downloaded from https://www.instagram.com/one23balloon/ on 2026-10-29 at the highest resolution Instagram served. Before launch the client must confirm permission for every row marked in the Faces column.

Most posts are Reels, so the file is the Reel's cover frame. `service-bouquets.jpg` is the first slide of a carousel and `sculpture-roses.jpg` is a feed photo. Rows from `gallery-13.jpg` onward also come from story highlights in an export of the client's account taken on 2026-10-30 (cited by highlight URL and story code) and from the first or third slide of two carousels. Files wider than 1440px were downscaled to 1440px wide (JPEG, mozjpeg, quality 86); every other file is the unmodified Instagram image.

Selection rules applied to every row: no recognisable customer face as the main subject, no customer brand names or logos, no licensed characters, no text overlays added in editing (this excludes the studio's own `one23balloon` watermark, which is burned into most posts from before 2026), sharp and well lit, widths of 1000px or more.
